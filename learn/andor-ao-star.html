Week 9 · Problem decomposition

AO*

Run it step by step

Some problems split into subproblems that must all be solved (AND), while others offer alternative ways to proceed (OR). AO* searches such AND–OR graphs for the cheapest solution graph, revising its choices as costs become known.

01Definition

Each node has connectors: a single-child connector is an OR option, a multi-child connector is an AND arc. Cost of a connector = its arc costs + sum of its children's costs. A node's cost is its cheapest connector, which is marked. AO* expands an unexpanded node on the marked partial solution, then revises costs and markings upwards.

02Pseudocode

repeat until start is SOLVED (or its cost exceeds FUTILITY)
    trace marked connectors from start; pick an unexpanded node n on that partial solution
    expand n: add its connectors and children (cost h); no connectors → cost FUTILITY
    S ← {n}
    while S not empty
        take m from S that has no descendant in S
        for each connector c of m: cost(c) = k(c) + Σ cost(children of c)
        cost(m) ← min over c;  mark the best connector
        if every child of the marked connector is SOLVED: label m SOLVED
        if cost(m) changed or m became SOLVED: add m's parents to S

03Line by line

trace marked connectors from the startThis is the current best partial solution.
expand one unexpanded node on it
revise costs bottom-upRecompute connector costs, mark the best, propagate to parents.
solved when the marked connector's children are all solvedRepeat until the start is solved.

04Formulas and why they hold

cost(c) = Σ over children t of (k + cost(t))

every arc of a connector costs k, plus its children

An AND arc needs all its children, so their costs add.

cost(n) = min over connectors c of n of cost(c)

choose the cheapest way to solve n

OR alternatives: only the best one is needed.

05Why it works, and when it does not

With admissible estimates AO* finds an optimal solution graph. The marked solution can change as estimates are corrected, which is visible in the trace.

07Traps

  • An AND connector costs the sum, not the minimum, of its children.
  • Propagate revisions to all ancestors whose marked connector uses the node.
