Tabu search

Run it step by step

Best-neighbour walking cycles because the best move is often the one that undoes the last. Tabu search adds a short-term memory: a variable that has just been flipped may not be flipped again for a few iterations, which forces the walk onward, even through worse or equal states.

⎙Tip: press Ctrl+P to print or save this page as PDF.

01Definition

At every iteration choose the best flip that is not tabu and take it even if h gets worse. The flipped variable becomes tabu for the tenure tt iterations. A tabu flip is still allowed when it gives an h larger than the best seen (the aspiration criterion). Optionally a long-term frequency memory subtracts λ times the number of past flips of a variable from its score, steering the search towards variables rarely flipped.

02Pseudocode

N ← start;  bestSeen ← N
for t = 1, 2, …
    if h(N) = m: stop
    rank the one-bit flips i by  score(i) = h(flip(N,i)) − λ · freq(i)    (ties: lowest bit)
    take the first flip that is not tabu,
         or is tabu but gives h > h(bestSeen)             # aspiration
    N ← flip(N,i);  tabu(i) ← t + tt;  freq(i) ← freq(i) + 1
    if h(N) > h(bestSeen): bestSeen ← N
return bestSeen

03Line by line

N ← start; bestSeen ← N
rank the flips by scoreScore is h of the neighbour; with λ > 0 subtract λ × (how often that variable has been flipped). Ties: lowest bit.
first flip that is not tabu, or tabu with h > bestSeenThis is the aspiration criterion: forbidding a move that would set a new record only blocks progress.
N ← the flip; tabu(i) ← t + ttVariable i cannot be flipped again at iterations t+1 … t+tt.
update bestSeen; return it at the endThe walk may leave a good string, so keep the best one.

04Formulas and why they hold

tabu(i) at iteration t ⇔ t ≤ tabuUntil(i), tabuUntil(i) = t₀ + tt

flipped at t₀, forbidden for the next tt iterations

Too short a tenure lets the walk cycle; too long blocks good moves. On the course example tt = 2 is enough to get out.

allow a tabu flip if h(new) > h(bestSeen)

aspiration criterion

Tabu exists to stop cycling, never to block a new record.

score(i) = h(flip(N,i)) − λ · freq(i)

frequency penalty (**supplementary**: the weight λ is a common implementation choice, not fixed by the course)

Long-term memory of how often each variable has been flipped diversifies the search; λ = 0 switches it off.

05Why it works, and when it does not

From 0000 on the course example: flip a (→ 1000, h 5); a is now tabu, and the best remaining flips tie at h 5, so b (the lowest bit) gives 1100, a sideways move; from 1100, a and b are tabu, and flipping d gives 1101, h 6, the unique solution. Hill climbing could never take the sideways move, and best-neighbour walking would have gone back to 0000. The tenure matters. On 'Local-maximum trap' (sat-local-trap) tenure 1 only forbids the immediate undo and the walk goes round 0000 → 0100 → 1100 → 1000 → 0000 and never finds a solution; tenure 2 breaks the loop and reaches 0111 in 5 flips. With tenure 4 and aspiration off every flip becomes tabu at 1111 and the search stops; with aspiration on, the tabu flip of a is allowed because 0111 (h 8) beats the best h seen (7).

07Traps

  • The tabu element is the variable (the move), not the whole string.
  • Tabu takes the best allowed move even when h decreases; only bestSeen is monotone.
  • Apply aspiration before rejecting a tabu flip.
  • With tenure 2 a variable flipped at iteration 1 is still tabu at iterations 2 and 3 and free again at 4.
  • Too short a tenure cycles (tenure 1 on sat-local-trap), too long a tenure freezes the search (tenure 4 with aspiration off stops when every flip is tabu). Aspiration is what rescues a record-breaking move from a long tenure.
