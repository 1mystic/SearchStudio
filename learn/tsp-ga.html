Genetic algorithm

Run it step by step

A genetic algorithm evolves a population of tours. Better tours are more likely to be chosen as parents, parents are combined by crossover, children are occasionally mutated, and the population slowly improves.

01Definition

Repeat for g generations: compute fitness, select N parents by roulette wheel (probability proportional to fitness), cross pairs, mutate with a small probability, and replace the weakest members with the strongest offspring.

02Pseudocode

P ← N random tours
repeat for g generations
    fitness(x) ← K / cost(x)
    S ← N roulette-wheel spins over P         (probability ∝ fitness)
    pair S as (1,2), (3,4), …; crossover each pair → offspring
    with probability pm mutate an offspring (swap two cities)
    replace the k weakest of P with the k strongest offspring
return best member of P

03Line by line

fitness(x) ← K / cost(x)TSP minimises cost, so fitness must grow as cost falls.
roulette wheel selectionSpin N times: each spin picks member i with probability fᵢ / Σ f.
crossover pairsUse a TSP-safe crossover so children are valid tours.
mutationSwap two cities with a small probability to keep diversity.
replace the k weakestKeeps the good members (elitism-like).

04Formulas and why they hold

pᵢ = fᵢ / Σⱼ fⱼ, fᵢ = K / costᵢ

share of the wheel for member i

Fitter members get bigger slices, but every member keeps some chance.

angleᵢ = 360° · pᵢ; pick i if cumᵢ₋₁ < u ≤ cumᵢ

spin u in [0,1) and see which slice it lands in

This is how the course traces roulette selection by hand.

05Why it works, and when it does not

Selection pushes toward good tours, crossover mixes their good parts, and mutation keeps exploring. Too much selection pressure makes the population identical too soon (premature convergence).

06Properties

completeno
optimalno
timeO(g · N · n)
spaceO(N · n)

07Traps

  • Fitness must be inverted for a minimisation problem.
  • If the population converges early: raise mutation, lower selection pressure, enlarge the population.
  • Cumulative probabilities decide roulette picks; recompute them carefully.
